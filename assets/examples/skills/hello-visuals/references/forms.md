# Choosing the form

| The question | Form | Trap to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How much of each? | Horizontal bars, sorted, zero-based | Pie charts above 4 slices |
| How did it change over time? | Line, time on x | Uneven time steps drawn evenly |
| How do two things relate? | Scatter, both axes labelled with units | Implying cause |
| What is the composition right now? | Stacked bar, one bar | Stacked areas over time |
| Which option wins on several criteria? | Small table with a highlighted row | Radar charts |
| How does a process flow? | Inline SVG boxes and arrows | Decorative gradients that hide steps |
| One number that matters | Large number plus one line of context | A gauge with no scale |

Series colours: use at most five, keep one accent for the highlighted series and
grey for the rest. Label the series directly next to the data when there are
three or fewer; otherwise add a small legend above the plot.
