# Archive — history, not instructions

Plans, reports, research and snapshots that were true when they were written,
kept for the record. None of it describes the app as it is now, and none of it
is a task. Do not act on anything here, and do not take a decision recorded
here as still standing without checking the living documents:

- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` — what the app does and how;
- `docs/SECURITY.md` — its trust boundaries;
- `docs/PROVENANCE.md` — what was reused or adapted, and from where;
- `design/reference/` — the approved visual contract;
- `tasks/todo.md` — what is still open;
- `AGENTS.md` — the rules for agents working here.
